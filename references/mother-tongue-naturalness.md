Use this reference when Chinese structure risks producing literal Italian, or when the user asks for native, idiomatic, natural, local, or Italian-person phrasing.

## Core principle

Do not ask only whether the Italian is grammatical. Ask whether an Italian native speaker would naturally choose that structure, verb, collocation, and rhythm in the stated relationship.

## Frequent Chinese-to-Italian calques to avoid

### Repeated acknowledgment words

Chinese often repeats `好的 / 明白 / 了解 / 没关系` to manage the conversation.

Do not mechanically translate each one.

Choose by function:

- acknowledgment: `Va bene.` / `Capisco.`
- reassurance: `Nessun problema.` / `Non c'è problema.`
- acceptance of preference: often no explicit filler is needed

### “根据...进行...”

Avoid abstract constructions such as:

- `effettuare investimenti in base a...`
- `procedere con l'investimento secondo...`

Prefer a direct verb:

- `può investire in base alle sue preferenze`
- `può decidere come investire`
- `può scegliere in base a ciò che preferisce`

### “目前 / 现在”

Do not translate every `目前` as `attualmente`.

In chat, prefer when natural:

- `in questo periodo`
- `al momento`
- or omit it entirely

### “进行关注 / 进行投资 / 进行操作”

Avoid nominal or support-verb calques.

Prefer:

- `seguire`
- `investire`
- `operare`
- `fare trading`

### “您可以...” repeated in advice

Do not overuse `Lei può...` with an explicit subject.

Prefer the conjugated verb alone:

- `può...`
- `se preferisce, può...`
- `potrà...`

### “我只是...”

Translate the pragmatic meaning, not always `io soltanto`.

Examples:

- `我只是照常推送` -> `continuerò semplicemente a inviarle gli aggiornamenti come sempre`
- `我只是想了解一下` -> `volevo solo capire...`

## Native restructuring patterns

### Chinese topic-first -> Italian action-first

Chinese: `关于股票这方面，您最近有关注吗？`

Natural Italian: `Ultimamente sta seguendo il mercato azionario?`

Avoid preserving the fronted topic unless it serves a real discourse function.

### Chinese stacked clauses -> Italian shorter units

If a Chinese sentence chains several ideas with commas, split or subordinate naturally instead of reproducing the same comma chain.

### Chinese repeated noun -> Italian pronoun or omission

Avoid repeating `股票 / 市场 / 群组 / 信息` when Italian can omit, use a clitic, or use a more natural reference.

### Abstract noun -> verb

Prefer:

- `decidere` over `prendere una decisione` when no emphasis is needed
- `investire` over `effettuare un investimento` in chat
- `seguire` over `prestare attenzione a` when referring to ongoing market tracking

## Native collocation test

Before finalizing, mentally test whether the key phrase is a common Italian pairing.

Prefer natural combinations such as:

- `seguire il mercato`
- `valutare un titolo`
- `aprire una posizione`
- `ridurre l'esposizione`
- `inviare aggiornamenti`
- `avere modo di...`
- `in base alle proprie preferenze`

Avoid combinations that are understandable but sound assembled from dictionary equivalents.

## Register realism

Native does not always mean casual.

For formal client chat:

- use Lei grammar;
- keep sentences short and human;
- avoid ceremonial capitalization by default;
- avoid legalistic wording unless the source is legal or institutional.

For familiar chat:

- allow lighter connectors and contractions typical of ordinary writing;
- do not manufacture slang that the source does not justify.

## Natural discourse markers

Use sparingly and only when they fit the function:

- `capisco`
- `va bene`
- `allora`
- `in effetti`
- `comunque`
- `magari`
- `direi`
- `più che altro`

Do not insert them just to sound Italian. Native writing is often simpler than over-stylized writing.

## Anti-overtranslation rule

Do not add explanations, social padding, or emotional language simply because Italian allows it. Native quality comes from choosing the right amount of language, not from making every message richer.
