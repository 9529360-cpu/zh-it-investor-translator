Use this reference only for explanation or reply-assistance mode.

## Principle

Separate explicit content from inference. Client messages are often short and ambiguous. Do not infer more enthusiasm, resistance, trust, or commitment than the wording supports.

## Common patterns

### Preference, not rejection

Italian examples:

- `Le cripto non sono strumenti che tratto.`
- `Preferisco operare solo sulle azioni.`
- `Non conosco molto il mondo delle criptovalute.`

Interpretation:

The person is narrowing the investment domain, not necessarily rejecting investment discussion in general.

Good reply direction:

Acknowledge the preference and keep future discussion focused on stocks.

Avoid:

Trying to persuade them into crypto immediately.

### Temporary disengagement

Example:

`È da un po' che non seguo il mercato azionario.`

Interpretation:

They have not followed the stock market recently. This does not by itself mean they have lost interest permanently.

Good reply direction:

Acknowledge naturally and, if useful, ask a light follow-up about what markets or sectors they follow now.

### Soft refusal

Examples:

- `Per ora preferisco di no.`
- `Non sono interessato in questo momento.`
- `Grazie, ma al momento passo.`

Interpretation:

Treat as a real refusal for now. Do not reframe it as hidden interest.

Good reply direction:

Respect the boundary and leave the door open without pressure.

### Lack of knowledge

Examples:

- `Non ne so molto.`
- `Non conosco bene questo settore.`

Interpretation:

This signals low familiarity, not automatically fear or rejection.

Good reply direction:

Keep explanations simple if asked; do not exploit uncertainty or overwhelm with claims.

### Busy / no time

Examples:

- `In questo periodo sono molto impegnato.`
- `Non ho molto tempo per seguire il mercato.`

Interpretation:

The obstacle is time unless the person says otherwise.

Good reply direction:

Keep future updates concise and optional.

## High-EQ reply rules

A good reply usually follows this order:

1. Acknowledge the actual point.
2. Validate the preference or situation without exaggeration.
3. Respond with one relevant next step or light question.
4. Keep pressure low.

Do not automatically add a question. If the other person has clearly closed the topic, a short acknowledgment may be better.

## Tone calibration

### Neutral client

Use calm, concise language.

### Friendly client

Allow slightly warmer phrasing and light conversational rhythm.

### Distant or terse client

Do not compensate with excessive warmth. Keep the reply short and respectful.

### Annoyed client

Acknowledge the issue, avoid defensiveness, and do not immediately pivot to promotion.

## Example reply patterns

Client: `Le cripto non sono strumenti che tratto.`

Chinese reply idea: `明白，每个人都有自己更熟悉的投资领域。那之后我会尽量以股票方面的信息为主。`

Italian version: `Capisco, ognuno ha i propri strumenti di riferimento. Allora cercherò di condividere soprattutto aggiornamenti legati alle azioni.`

Client: `È da un po' che non seguo il mercato azionario.`

Chinese reply idea: `明白，最近没有关注也很正常。您现在主要关注其他市场，还是暂时没有做投资？`

Italian version: `Capisco. In questo periodo sta seguendo altri mercati oppure si è semplicemente presa una pausa dagli investimenti?`

Client: `Per ora non sono interessato.`

Chinese reply idea: `好的，明白，我尊重您的想法。之后如果您有需要，随时联系我就好。`

Italian version: `Va bene, capisco e rispetto la sua scelta. Se in futuro ne avrà bisogno, mi può scrivere quando vuole.`
