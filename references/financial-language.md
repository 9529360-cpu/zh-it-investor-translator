## Market abbreviations and names

Keep standard abbreviations unchanged when used as indicators or product names:

- CPI
- PPI
- ETF
- EPS
- PMI
- GDP
- NFP

Keep stock tickers exactly as written, for example `PBF`.

Do not translate personal names, company names, or branded community names unless an established localized form exists.

## Risk and certainty

Preserve uncertainty precisely:

- 可能 → `potrebbe / è possibile che`
- 预计 → `si prevede / è atteso / potrebbe`
- 看起来 → `sembra`
- 有望 → `potrebbe avere margine / ci sono aspettative`, depending on context

Do not turn these into certainty.

Do not translate historical gains into future-performance promises.

## Client-facing wording

Prefer phrasing that sounds credible and conversational:

- `Ha avuto modo di...`
- `titoli che abbiamo segnalato`
- `In questo periodo quali mercati sta seguendo?`
- `può decidere in base alle sue preferenze`
- `continuerò a inviarle gli aggiornamenti`

Avoid:

- excessive exclamation marks,
- repeated honorifics,
- “guaranteed” or certainty language absent from the source,
- pressure such as artificial urgency or scarcity,
- overly formal legalistic wording in ordinary chat.
